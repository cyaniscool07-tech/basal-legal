Green lentil salad

Mustard dressing, carrots and red onion. 100% plant-based, can be made ahead.

Ingredients

Makes 2 servings.

Allergens: Mustard, Sulphites. Worked out from the ingredients; check the labels of the products you use.

Method

  1. Cook the lentils for 25 minutes, drain and let them cool slightly.

  2. Grate the carrots, slice the onion and chop the parsley.

  3. Whisk the mustard, vinegar and oil, then toss everything together.
