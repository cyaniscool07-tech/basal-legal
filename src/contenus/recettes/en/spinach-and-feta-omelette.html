Spinach and feta omelette

Three eggs and greens, ready in ten minutes. 26 g of protein per serving, ready in 9 min, vegetarian.

Ingredients

Makes 1 serving.

Allergens: Eggs, Milk. Worked out from the ingredients; check the labels of the products you use.

Method

  1. Wilt the spinach in the hot oil for 1 minute.

  2. Beat the eggs and pour them over the spinach.

  3. Crumble in the feta, let it set over a low heat, then fold.
