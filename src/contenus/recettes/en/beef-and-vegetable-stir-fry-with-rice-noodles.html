Beef and vegetable stir-fry with rice noodles

Seared over a high heat, with crunchy vegetables. 30 g of protein per serving.

Ingredients

Makes 3 servings.

Allergens: Gluten, Sesame, Soya. Worked out from the ingredients; check the labels of the products you use.

Method

  1. Soak the noodles in hot water for 5 minutes, then drain.

  2. Sear the beef strips in very hot oil for 2 minutes, then set aside.

  3. Stir-fry the vegetables with the garlic and ginger for 4 minutes, then add the noodles, beef and soy sauce.
