Vegan chilli

The plant-based version: just as hearty, on a budget. 100% plant-based, can be made ahead.

Ingredients

Makes 5 servings.

Method

  1. Soften the onion and pepper in the oil.

  2. Add the garlic, spices, tomatoes, lentils and 300 ml of water; simmer for 20 minutes.

  3. Add the beans and sweetcorn, cook for 10 more minutes and serve with the cooked rice.
