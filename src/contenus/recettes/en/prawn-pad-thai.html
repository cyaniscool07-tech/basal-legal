Prawn pad thai

Stir-fried rice noodles with egg, peanuts and lime. 36 g of protein per serving.

Ingredients

Makes 2 servings.

Allergens: Crustaceans, Eggs, Gluten, Peanuts, Soya. Worked out from the ingredients; check the labels of the products you use.

Method

  1. Soak the noodles in hot water for 5 minutes and drain.

  2. Stir-fry the onion and prawns in the oil for 3 minutes, push them aside and scramble the eggs next to them.

  3. Add the noodles, soy sauce and sugar, toss for 2 minutes, then add the crushed peanuts and lime.
