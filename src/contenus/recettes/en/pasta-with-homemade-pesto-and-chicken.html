Pasta with homemade pesto and chicken

Basil and almond pesto with cherry tomatoes. 49 g of protein per serving.

Ingredients

Makes 2 servings.

Allergens: Gluten, Milk, Tree nuts. Worked out from the ingredients; check the labels of the products you use.

Method

  1. Blend the basil, Parmesan, almonds, garlic and oil into a pesto.

  2. Cook the pasta and pan-fry the sliced chicken for 8 minutes.

  3. Toss the pasta, chicken, pesto and halved cherry tomatoes together.
