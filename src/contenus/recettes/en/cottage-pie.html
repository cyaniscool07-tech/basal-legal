Cottage pie

Homemade mash and lean beef, browned in the oven. 31 g of protein per serving, can be made ahead.

Ingredients

Makes 4 servings.

Allergens: Milk. Worked out from the ingredients; check the labels of the products you use.

Method

  1. Boil the potatoes for 20 minutes, then mash them with the milk and butter.

  2. Cook the onion and beef for 8 minutes.

  3. Spread the meat in a dish, top with the mash and cheese and bake for 20 minutes at 200 °C.
