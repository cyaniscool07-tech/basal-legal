Chicken and salad wraps

With a fromage frais and mustard dressing; easy to take with you. 32 g of protein per serving, ready in 18 min.

Ingredients

Makes 2 servings.

Allergens: Gluten, Milk, Mustard. Worked out from the ingredients; check the labels of the products you use.

Method

  1. Pan-fry the chicken strips for 8 minutes.

  2. Mix the fromage frais and mustard.

  3. Fill the tortillas with the dressing, lettuce, tomato and chicken, then roll them up.
