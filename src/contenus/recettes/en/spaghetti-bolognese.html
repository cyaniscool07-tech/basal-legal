Spaghetti bolognese

A slow-cooked sauce with lean beef and vegetables. 40 g of protein per serving, can be made ahead.

Ingredients

Makes 4 servings.

Allergens: Gluten, Milk. Worked out from the ingredients; check the labels of the products you use.

Method

  1. Soften the chopped onion and carrot in the oil, then brown the beef.

  2. Add the garlic and passata; simmer for 25 minutes.

  3. Cook the pasta, toss with the sauce and serve with Parmesan.
