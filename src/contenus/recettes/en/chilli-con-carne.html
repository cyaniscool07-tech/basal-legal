Chilli con carne

Beef, kidney beans and spices, served with rice. 34 g of protein per serving, can be made ahead.

Ingredients

Makes 5 servings.

Method

  1. Soften the onion and pepper in the oil, then add the beef and brown it.

  2. Add the garlic, spices, tomatoes and beans; simmer for 30 minutes.

  3. Cook the rice meanwhile and serve together.
