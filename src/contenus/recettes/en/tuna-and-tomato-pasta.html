Ingredients

Makes 2 servings.

Allergens: Fish, Gluten, Milk. Worked out from the ingredients; check the labels of the products you use.

Method

  1. Cook the pasta.

  2. Soften the onion in the oil, add the passata and simmer for 8 minutes.

  3. Add the flaked tuna, toss with the pasta and serve with Parmesan.
