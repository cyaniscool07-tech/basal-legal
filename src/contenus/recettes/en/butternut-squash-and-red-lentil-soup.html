Butternut squash and red lentil soup

Silky, and filling thanks to the lentils. 100% plant-based, can be made ahead.

Ingredients

Makes 4 servings.

Method

  1. Soften the onion in the oil with the cumin.

  2. Add the diced squash, the lentils and 900 ml of water; cook for 25 minutes.

  3. Blend with the coconut milk.
