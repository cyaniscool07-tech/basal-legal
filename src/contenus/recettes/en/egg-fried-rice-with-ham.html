Egg fried rice with ham

Ideal with leftover rice from the day before. Ready in 20 min.

Ingredients

Makes 3 servings.

Allergens: Eggs, Gluten, Soya. Worked out from the ingredients; check the labels of the products you use.

Method

  1. Cook the rice and let it cool (ideally the day before).

  2. Scramble the eggs in the oil, then add the onion, diced ham and peas.

  3. Add the rice and soy sauce and stir-fry for 4 minutes over a high heat.
