Spanish omelette

A thick potato omelette, eaten hot or cold. Vegetarian, can be made ahead.

Ingredients

Makes 3 servings.

Allergens: Eggs. Worked out from the ingredients; check the labels of the products you use.

Method

  1. Cook the thinly sliced potatoes and onion in the oil, covered, for 20 minutes.

  2. Beat the eggs and stir in the vegetables.

  3. Cook over a low heat for 5 minutes, flip using a plate and cook for 3 more minutes.
