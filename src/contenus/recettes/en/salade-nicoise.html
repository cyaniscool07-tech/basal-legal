Salade niçoise

Tuna, eggs, green beans and potatoes. 29 g of protein per serving.

Ingredients

Makes 2 servings.

Allergens: Eggs, Fish, Sulphites. Worked out from the ingredients; check the labels of the products you use.

Method

  1. Boil the potatoes for 20 minutes, the beans for 8 minutes and the eggs for 9 minutes.

  2. Chop the tomatoes and potatoes; peel and quarter the eggs.

  3. Assemble with the tuna and olives and dress with the oil and vinegar.
