Homemade apple and almond muesli

Ingredients

Makes 1 serving.

Allergens: Gluten, Milk, Tree nuts, Sulphites. Worked out from the ingredients; check the labels of the products you use.

Method

  1. Roughly chop the almonds.

  2. Grate the apple, skin on.

  3. Mix everything with the oats and raisins, then pour over the milk.
