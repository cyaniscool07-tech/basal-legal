Salmon poke bowl

Rice, salmon, avocado and cucumber, Hawaiian style. 39 g of protein per serving.

Ingredients

Makes 1 serving.

Allergens: Fish, Gluten, Sesame, Soya. Worked out from the ingredients; check the labels of the products you use.

Method

  1. Cook the rice and let it cool slightly.

  2. Dice the salmon and marinate it in the soy sauce for 5 minutes.

  3. Arrange the rice, salmon, sliced avocado and cucumber, then sprinkle with sesame.
