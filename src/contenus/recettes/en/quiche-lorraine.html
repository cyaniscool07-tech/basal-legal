Quiche lorraine

The traditional recipe, with bacon and Emmental. Can be made ahead.

Ingredients

Makes 6 servings.

Allergens: Eggs, Gluten, Milk. Worked out from the ingredients; check the labels of the products you use.

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 190 °C and line a tin with the pastry.

  2. Dry-fry the lardons and scatter them over the pastry with the cheese.

  3. Beat the eggs, milk and cream, pour in and bake for 35 minutes.
