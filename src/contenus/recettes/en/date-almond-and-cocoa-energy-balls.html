Date, almond and cocoa energy balls

Ten no-bake bites for training days. Ready in 15 min, 100% plant-based, can be made ahead.

Ingredients

Makes 10 servings.

Allergens: Gluten, Tree nuts. Worked out from the ingredients; check the labels of the products you use.

Method

  1. Blitz the almonds and oats into a coarse powder.

  2. Add the dates and cocoa and blend into a paste.

  3. Roll into ten balls and keep them in the fridge.
