Chicken fajitas

Spiced chicken, peppers and onions; fill them at the table. 31 g of protein per serving.

Ingredients

Makes 4 servings.

Allergens: Gluten, Milk. Worked out from the ingredients; check the labels of the products you use.

Method

  1. Slice the chicken, peppers and onion into strips.

  2. Stir-fry the chicken with the spices in the oil for 6 minutes, then add the vegetables for 5 minutes.

  3. Warm the tortillas and fill them, using the fromage frais as a dip.
