Chicken and vegetable couscous

Cumin-scented broth, chickpeas and tender vegetables. 36 g of protein per serving, can be made ahead.

Ingredients

Makes 4 servings.

Allergens: Gluten. Worked out from the ingredients; check the labels of the products you use.

Method

  1. Brown the chicken and onion in the oil and add the cumin.

  2. Add the carrots, tomatoes and 700 ml of water; cook for 20 minutes, then add the courgettes and chickpeas for 10 minutes.

  3. Pour 300 ml of boiling water over the couscous, cover for 5 minutes and fluff with a fork.
