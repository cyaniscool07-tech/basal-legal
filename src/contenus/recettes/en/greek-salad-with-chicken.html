Greek salad with chicken

Raw vegetables, feta and grilled chicken, ready in fifteen minutes. 45 g of protein per serving, ready in 18 min.

Ingredients

Makes 1 serving.

Allergens: Milk. Worked out from the ingredients; check the labels of the products you use.

Method

  1. Grill the chicken for 8 minutes, then slice it.

  2. Chop the vegetables and thinly slice the onion.

  3. Add the feta, olives, oil and lemon, then the chicken.
