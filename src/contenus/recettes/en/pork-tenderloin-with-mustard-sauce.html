Pork tenderloin with mustard sauce

Creamy sauce, green beans and potatoes. 28 g of protein per serving.

Ingredients

Makes 4 servings.

Allergens: Milk, Mustard. Worked out from the ingredients; check the labels of the products you use.

Method

  1. Boil the potatoes for 20 minutes and the green beans for 10 minutes.

  2. Sear the pork medallions in the oil for 4 minutes on each side.

  3. Add the mustard and cream to the pan, let it thicken for 2 minutes and pour over the meat.
