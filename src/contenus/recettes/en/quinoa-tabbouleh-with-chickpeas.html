Quinoa tabbouleh with chickpeas

Fresh herbs, lemon and raw vegetables; keeps for two days. 100% plant-based, can be made ahead.

Ingredients

Makes 3 servings.

Method

  1. Cook the quinoa for 12 minutes, drain and let it cool.

  2. Finely dice the cucumber and tomatoes and chop the herbs.

  3. Mix everything with the chickpeas, lemon juice and oil.
