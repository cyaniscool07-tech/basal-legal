Ratatouille

Slow-cooked summer vegetables, even better the next day. 100% plant-based, can be made ahead.

Ingredients

Makes 4 servings.

Method

  1. Dice all the vegetables.

  2. Soften the onion and aubergine in the oil, then add the courgettes and pepper.

  3. Add the tomatoes, garlic and herbs; simmer, covered, for 40 minutes.
