Cod with rice and summer vegetables

Flaky white fish with courgettes and cherry tomatoes. 36 g of protein per serving.

Ingredients

Makes 2 servings.

Allergens: Fish. Worked out from the ingredients; check the labels of the products you use.

Method

  1. Cook the rice.

  2. Pan-fry the sliced courgettes and cherry tomatoes in the oil for 8 minutes.

  3. Lay the cod on top, cover for 8 minutes, then add the lemon and parsley.
