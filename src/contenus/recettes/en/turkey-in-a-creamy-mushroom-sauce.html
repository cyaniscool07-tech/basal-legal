Turkey in a creamy mushroom sauce

Ingredients

Makes 4 servings.

Allergens: Milk. Worked out from the ingredients; check the labels of the products you use.

Method

  1. Cook the rice.

  2. Pan-fry the turkey strips in the oil for 6 minutes, then set aside.

  3. Soften the onion and mushrooms, add the cream and the turkey and simmer for 3 minutes.
