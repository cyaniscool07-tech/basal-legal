Baked salmon, sweet potato and green beans

Ingredients

Makes 2 servings.

Allergens: Fish. Worked out from the ingredients; check the labels of the products you use.

Method

  1. Dice the sweet potato, toss it with the oil and roast for 15 minutes at 200 °C.

  2. Add the green beans and salmon fillets to the tray.

  3. Roast for 15 more minutes and squeeze over the lemon before serving.
