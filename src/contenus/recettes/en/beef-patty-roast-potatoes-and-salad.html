Beef patty, roast potatoes and salad

A French bistro classic, made at home. 33 g of protein per serving.

2 servingsPrep 10 minCook 35 minTotal 45 minFrench cuisine

Ingredients

Makes 2 servings.

Method

  1. Cut the potatoes into wedges, toss them with the oil and herbs and roast for 35 minutes at 200 °C.

  2. Shape two patties and cook them for 3 minutes on each side.

  3. Serve with the salad.
