Mushroom risotto

Creamy with Parmesan, without any cream. Vegetarian.

Ingredients

Makes 4 servings.

Allergens: Celery, Milk. Worked out from the ingredients; check the labels of the products you use.

Method

  1. Dissolve the stock powder in 1 litre of hot water.

  2. Soften the onion and mushrooms in the oil, then add the rice and toast it for 2 minutes.

  3. Add the stock a ladle at a time, stirring, for 20 minutes, then stir in the butter and Parmesan.
