Shakshuka

Eggs poached in a spiced tomato and pepper sauce. Vegetarian.

Ingredients

Makes 2 servings.

Allergens: Eggs. Worked out from the ingredients; check the labels of the products you use.

Method

  1. Soften the sliced onion and pepper in the oil for 5 minutes.

  2. Add the garlic, spices and tomatoes; simmer for 10 minutes.

  3. Make four wells, crack in the eggs and cover until the whites are set.
