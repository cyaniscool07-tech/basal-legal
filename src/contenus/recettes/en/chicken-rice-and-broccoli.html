Chicken, rice and broccoli

The meal-prep classic for three meals; cooked rice keeps 24 hours in the fridge. 46 g of protein per serving, can be made ahead.

Ingredients

Makes 3 servings.

Allergens: Gluten, Soya. Worked out from the ingredients; check the labels of the products you use.

Method

  1. Cook the rice according to the packet.

  2. Brown the diced chicken in the oil for 8 to 10 minutes, then add the soy sauce.

  3. Steam the broccoli for 6 minutes and divide everything between three boxes.
