Banana bread

A good use for overripe bananas. Vegetarian, can be made ahead.

Ingredients

Makes 8 servings.

Allergens: Eggs, Gluten, Milk. Worked out from the ingredients; check the labels of the products you use.

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 180 °C.

  2. Mash the bananas, then add the eggs, sugar, oil and milk.

  3. Fold in the flour and baking powder, pour into a loaf tin and bake for 50 minutes.
