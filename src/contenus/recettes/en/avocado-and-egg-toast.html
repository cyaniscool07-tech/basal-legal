Avocado and egg toast

Wholemeal toast, smashed avocado and an egg. Ready in 11 min, vegetarian.

Ingredients

Makes 1 serving.

Allergens: Eggs, Gluten. Worked out from the ingredients; check the labels of the products you use.

Method

  1. Cook the egg your way: soft-boiled (6 minutes in boiling water) or fried in a non-stick pan without any fat.

  2. Toast the bread and mash the avocado with the lemon.

  3. Spread it on the toast and top with the egg.
