Chia and mango overnight oats

Ingredients

Makes 1 serving.

Allergens: Gluten, Milk. Worked out from the ingredients; check the labels of the products you use.

Method

  1. Mix the oats, chia, milk and fromage frais in a jar.

  2. Leave in the fridge overnight.

  3. Add the diced mango just before serving.
