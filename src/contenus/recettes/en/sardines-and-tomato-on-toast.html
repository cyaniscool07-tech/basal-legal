Sardines and tomato on toast

Omega-3s on wholemeal toast in five minutes. Ready in 7 min.

Ingredients

Makes 2 servings.

Allergens: Fish, Gluten. Worked out from the ingredients; check the labels of the products you use.

Method

  1. Toast the bread.

  2. Mash the sardines with the lemon juice.

  3. Spread on the toast and top with sliced tomato.
