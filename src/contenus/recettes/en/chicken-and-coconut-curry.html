Chicken and coconut curry

Chicken and pepper in a spiced coconut sauce, with rice. 37 g of protein per serving, can be made ahead.

Ingredients

Makes 4 servings.

Allergens: Celery, Mustard. Worked out from the ingredients; check the labels of the products you use.

Method

  1. Brown the diced chicken in the oil, then set aside.

  2. Soften the onion, pepper, ginger and curry powder; add the tomatoes and coconut milk.

  3. Return the chicken, simmer for 15 minutes and serve with the rice.
