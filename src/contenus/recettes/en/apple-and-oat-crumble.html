Apple and oat crumble

Ingredients

Makes 5 servings.

Allergens: Gluten, Milk. Worked out from the ingredients; check the labels of the products you use.

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 180 °C and dice the apples into a dish with the cinnamon.

  2. Rub the flour, oats, sugar and butter together with your fingertips.

  3. Scatter over the apples and bake for 30 minutes.
