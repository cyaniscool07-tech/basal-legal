Egg and vegetable muffins

Make ahead: three days in the fridge, the rest in the freezer. Vegetarian, can be made ahead.

Ingredients

Makes 6 servings.

Allergens: Eggs, Milk. Worked out from the ingredients; check the labels of the products you use.

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 180 °C.

  2. Beat the eggs with the milk, then add the diced pepper, chopped spinach and cheese.

  3. Divide between six muffin moulds and bake for 20 minutes.
