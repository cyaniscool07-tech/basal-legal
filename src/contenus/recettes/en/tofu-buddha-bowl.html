Tofu buddha bowl

Quinoa, roasted sweet potato, red cabbage and avocado. 25 g of protein per serving, 100% plant-based.

Ingredients

Makes 2 servings.

Allergens: Gluten, Sesame, Soya. Worked out from the ingredients; check the labels of the products you use.

Method

  1. Roast the diced sweet potato with the oil for 25 minutes at 200 °C.

  2. Cook the quinoa and pan-fry the diced tofu with the soy sauce.

  3. Arrange everything with the shredded red cabbage and avocado, then sprinkle with sesame.
