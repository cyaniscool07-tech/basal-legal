What the app does

Four kinds of tracking that talk to each other.

Most apps keep the plate and the gym apart. Basal puts them side by side, and uses both to correct its own estimates.

01

Nutrition

Calories and macros per meal, food search in the Ciqual table and barcode scanning.

02

Metabolism

An expenditure that starts from the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, then corrects itself with your actual weight change.

Adaptive metabolism (in French)
03

Strength training

Over 870 exercises, sets, loads and records, shown next to your weight trend.

04

Meals

50 calculated recipes, a weekly plan, a shopping list and a cooking mode.

Recipes (in French)
