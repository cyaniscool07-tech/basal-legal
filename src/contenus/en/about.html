About

Who makes Basal

Basal is a nutrition, weight and strength-training tracking app. It is designed, developed and maintained by Théo Serrano, an independent developer in France.

The publisher

Théo Serrano publishes Basal as a sole trader (entrepreneur individuel), under the trade name Basal. He writes the app, this site and the articles on it, and answers messages himself. Full contact details are in the legal notice.

The principles

  • Start from a published formula, then correct it with your data. The starting expenditure comes from the Mifflin-St Jeor equation; the app then checks it against your meals and how your weight changes. All the calculations are detailed on the Method page.
  • Say when we do not know. As long as the data is too thin, the app shows “not enough data” rather than a number that would look certain.
  • Keep the essentials free. Tracking meals, weight and sessions, and the recipes, require neither a subscription nor an account.
  • Do not use health as a selling point. Basal observes, calculates and explains; it is not a medical device, and the app says so.

What Basal lives on

An optional Premium subscription, sold through Google Play and the App Store, which adds analyses and assistants (see what is free and what is Premium). Basal shows no ads and sells no data.
