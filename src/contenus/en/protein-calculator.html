Why a reference weight

Protein needs follow lean mass, not total weight. Applying 2.2 g per kilo at 120 kg would give 264 g a day, over 1,000 kcal before counting anything else: no recommendation supports such a figure. Up to a BMI of 25, the reference weight is therefore your actual weight; above it, only a quarter of the excess counts. This is the adjusted-weight formula, the standard approximation when lean mass is not measured.

And the rest of the plate

Protein is only part of the calculation. The macro calculator adds fat and carbs, with the same rules, and the calculated recipes show their protein per serving.

What this calculation does not know

It is an order of magnitude. It rests on the usual ranges in sports nutrition, not on a measurement of your body composition.

Some situations call for a health professional: kidney disease, pregnancy, a prescribed diet, an eating disorder.
