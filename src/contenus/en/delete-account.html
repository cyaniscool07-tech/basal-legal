Delete your account — Basal

Last updated: 7 August 2026

This is a translation of the French version. If the two versions ever differ, only the French version is authoritative.

This page explains how to delete your Basal account (nutrition, weight, metabolism and activity tracking) and the data linked to it. There are two ways: from the app, or by email if you no longer have it.

Worth remembering: you do not need an account to use Basal. If you never created one, there is nothing to delete — none of your data has left your phone, and uninstalling the app erases it.

1. From the app (immediate)

  1. Open Basal.
  2. Go to Settings (the gear at the top right of any tab).
  3. Open Account (“Account, backup and Premium status”).
  4. Scroll down to “Delete my account”.
  5. Confirm.

Deletion is immediate and permanent. No request to send, no waiting period, no approval on our side.

2. By email (if you no longer have the app)

If you have uninstalled Basal or changed phone, write to contact@basal-app.fr from your account’s email address, with the subject “Account deletion”.

Writing from the account’s address is what lets us check that the request really comes from you: it is the only protection against someone asking for another person’s account to be deleted. If you no longer have access to that address, say so in your message and we will find another way to verify you.

The request is handled within 30 days at most, and in practice much faster.

3. What is deleted, and what is not

Data Becomes
Your account and your email address Deleted permanently
Your data backed up on the server (profile, weight, meals, recipes, sessions, targets) Deleted permanently
The data still on your phone Kept — the app goes back to being local, as before the account was created. To erase it too, uninstall the app or clear its data from your phone’s settings.
Your Premium subscription, if you have one Not cancelled. A subscription belongs to your Google Play account or your Apple ID, not to your Basal account — we have no way of cancelling it for you. See below.

No copy is kept after deletion: there is no grace period, no bin, no archive. A deleted account cannot be restored, and the data it held is lost. If you want to keep it, export it first from Settings → My data (export as JSON or CSV).

4. Cancelling a Premium subscription

Cancellation is done entirely with the platform where you subscribed, never in the app:

  • Android — Play Store → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Basal → Cancel.
  • iPhone — phone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Basal → Cancel Subscription.

Deleting your Basal account without cancelling would let the subscription renew. Do both if that is what you want.

5. Questions

For any question about deleting your account or about how your data is handled: contact@basal-app.fr

What is collected, why and where, is detailed in the privacy policy.
